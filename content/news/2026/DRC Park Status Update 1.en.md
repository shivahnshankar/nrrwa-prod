{{< initiative title="Fencing" image="/assets/images/news/2026/drc_park/1.jpg" align="right" >}}
Fencing on two sides of park was needed to protect the plants from stray cattle. Work completed, sans painting.
{{< /initiative >}}

{{< initiative title="Walking Path" image="/assets/images/news/2026/drc_park/2.jpg" align="left" >}}
Walking path marked, soil upto 3 inches removed and gravel added except in few patches. Rolling work is pending.
{{< /initiative >}}

{{< initiative title="Repairs" image="/assets/images/news/2026/drc_park/3.jpg" align="right" >}}
Earlier civil structures are repaired wherever necessary. Water ways are filled with red soil for plating flower saplings.
{{< /initiative >}}

{{< initiative title="Bridge" image="/assets/images/news/2026/drc_park/4.jpg" align="left" >}}
Japanese bridge, step and railings repaired. Structures need painting
{{< /initiative >}}

{{< initiative title="Cleaning" image="/assets/images/news/2026/drc_park/5.jpg" align="right" >}}
Major work of clearing the shrubs is over.
{{< /initiative >}}
