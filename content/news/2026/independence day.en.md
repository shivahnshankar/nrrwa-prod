NRRWA successfully organized its 3rd consecutive Blood Donation camp as part of Independence Day celebration. Residents participated enthusiastically in this camp and 30 people donated blood.

**Dr. Kusuma** and her team from K.R. Hospital Blood bank and Mysore Medical College (MMC) rendered their services successfully. The program began with a welcome address by NRRWA President **Mr. M.N.Narasimha Murthy**. A detailed awareness talk on the importance of organ donation was delivered by **Mr. Vijaya and Mr Chandu Patel from Mysore Medical College( SOTTO)**. Roopanagar residents attended the session and actively interacted and many of them pledged and registered for organ donation.

The program was coordinated by **Dr.Sushma Appaiah** Director NRRWA, health and wellness committee. NRRWA office bearers and Executive committee members actively participated in the program. Joint secretary **Mrs. Priya Shashidharan** proposed vote of thanks.
